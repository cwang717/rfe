\section{Executive Summary of the RFE Response}

USCIS has accepted that I satisfy the judging criterion and the authorship criterion. The central issue in this response is whether my work satisfies the original-contributions criterion and, in the final merits analysis, whether the totality of my record shows sustained acclaim and recognition at the top of the field.

The evidence below is organized around three field-level contributions:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item I produced real-world evidence showing how human drivers behave around autonomous vehicles.
  \item I created a more realistic framework for predicting and simulating uncertain human driving behavior.
  \item I developed planning, control, and learning-based methods that help autonomous vehicles move more safely and efficiently in mixed traffic.
\end{enumerate}

These contributions are not presented as mere publications. My publications disclosed the contributions, but the contributions themselves are the underlying datasets, empirical findings, analytical frameworks, control methods, and autonomous-driving insights that others later adopted, extended, benchmarked, relied upon, or used to guide practical work.
